Privacy policy: Tel Baati (तेल बाती)

Last updated: 24 November 2026

हिन्दी

This policy is about the Android app Tel Baati, made in India by Gopu studio. “We” means Gopu studio. We do not collect any information about you through the app. It has no accounts, and we have no server. This policy says what information the app uses, who gets it, and what you can do.

In short

  • We ourselves do not collect any information about you, and keep none on a server. What the app saves is kept in the app, on your phone.
  • Your games, stats and settings are not sent to us.
  • Google collects some information through the app to show ads. We do not receive it.
  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos or exact location, and we do not sell your information for money.
  • About you, we see only what you write to us, and what Google Play tells us, such as the order of a purchase. The details are below.
  • If you have any question, write to us: connect2gopu@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

Your games, results, streak, settings, counts that limit how often ads are shown, your answer about ads, and whether ads are removed. This is not sent to us.

It is not put in Android's cloud backup. Some phones may copy it when you move directly to a new phone. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, or when you clear the app's storage in your phone's settings.

When you share a result, it goes to the app you choose. The picture and the text contain your result only.

What Google collects to show ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app collects and shares:

  • your IP address, from which your general location can be estimated
  • how you use the app and the ads, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • technical information, such as launch time, hangs and energy use
  • device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID

This goes to Google and may be shared with Google's advertising partners. Depending on your choices and the law where you live, it may also be used to choose ads that match your interests. Google may keep it in other countries. We cannot see this information about any one person; we see only totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Google's software may collect this also after you have paid to remove ads, because watching an ad for extra oil stays possible.

How Google uses this information

Google Privacy Policy

Your choices about ads

  • Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested. There you can change your answer at any time under Settings, Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Google, Ads. The place can differ between phones.
  • You can play without internet. Ads are then not loaded.

Purchase

If you pay to remove ads, Google Play takes the payment. The app asks Google Play on your phone whether the purchase exists, and keeps the answer on your phone. We never see your card or bank details. Google gives us a record of the order for accounts and refunds.

Google Payments Privacy Notice

What Google Play shows us

Google Play shows us totals, such as how many people installed the app. If you allowed it on your phone, it also shows us reports when the app stops or hangs, with the phone model and the place of the fault, but not your name. A review you write on Play, and the name shown with it, can be seen by everyone, and by us.

Reminder

The daily reminder is off until you turn it on. It is made on your phone, and no message comes from a server. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

When you write to us

If you send an email, we see your address and what you wrote. Our email is kept by Google (Gmail). We use it only to answer you and to improve the app.

Why this information may be used

  • Ads: with your consent where the law requires it. Elsewhere, because ads keep the app free.
  • Purchase: to give you what you bought, and to keep accounts as the law requires.
  • Fault reports: so that we can repair the app.
  • Emails: because you asked or told us something.

Who gets the information

Google, for ads, Play, payments and email, and Google's partners for ads. We give information to no one else, unless the law requires it.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

  • On your phone: until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Emails: at most 1 year after the matter is settled. If you ask, we delete them sooner.
  • Order records: as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • What Google holds: Google decides how long. Use your Google account and the advertising ID settings to control it.

Your rights

As the law where you live provides, you can ask us:

  • to see the information we hold about you, or to get a copy of it
  • to correct or delete it
  • to stop, when you withdraw consent or object
  • to accept a person you name, who can ask for this if you die or are unable to

Write to connect2gopu@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days, free of charge. We may need to ask you something to find your email or order. For information that only Google holds, please ask Google.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live. In India this is the Data Protection Board of India, once it accepts complaints.

In some US states, giving information for ads that match your interests can count as “selling” or “sharing” it. Where the law lets you refuse, you can do so under Settings, Ad privacy choices.

Safety

The app's data is kept in its private storage, which other apps cannot normally read. Information sent to Google is encrypted on the way. Still, no method is completely safe.

Children

The app is for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If a child has sent us information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text and its date will be in the app and on our website. The website has the newest text.
